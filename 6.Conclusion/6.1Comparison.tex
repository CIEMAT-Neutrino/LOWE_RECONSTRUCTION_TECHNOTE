\subsection{\acrlong{fd} Comparison\label{sec:GEOMETRY_COMPARISON}}
So far we have used as reference the \textit{hd\_1x2x6\_centralAPA} geometry, or compared between the \textit{hd\_1x2x6\_lateralAPA} and the \textit{vd\_1x8x14\_3view\_30deg\_nominal}. In this section, we compare all geometry and background configurations which are described in \cref{sec:GEOMETRY}.

In the case of the vertex resolution, we can use the previously defined figure of merit and quote the percent of events with a vertex error below a certain threshold. The results are shown in \cref{tab:MATCHING_COMPARISON}.

\begin{figure}[ht]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Images/marley_official_neutrino_energy_resolution_driftreco_nhits3_totalenergy_selectedenergy_solarenergy.png}
    \caption[width=1\linewidth]{(left) \textbf{TotalEnergy} resolution comparison. (center) \textbf{SelectedEnergy} resolution comparison. (right) \textbf{SolarEnergy} resolution comparison between geometries.\label{fig:ENERGY_RESOLUTION_COMPARISON}}
\end{figure}

Applying the \textbf{TotalEnergy} reconstruction algorithm, we can see that the energy resolution is better in the hd\_1x2x6\_centralAPA geometry, which is expected since it has a lower background rate and smaller drift volume causing therefore less diffusion. The energy resolution is shown in \cref{fig:ENERGY_RESOLUTION_COMPARISON} (left).

Applying the \textbf{SelectedEnergy} reconstruction algorithm, we can see that the energy resolution is again better in the \gls{hd} geometry for the same reasons as before. But this time, the difference is less pronounced due to the fact that the \textbf{SelectedEnergy} algorithm maximizes the adjacent cluster significance.

Finally, applying the \textbf{SolarEnergy} reconstruction algorithm and the appropriate drift correction, we obtain the most similar results. This comes at the expense of a slightly worse energy resolution above \SI{20}{\mega\electronvolt}. 

We can see that the energy resolution is similar between the \textit{hd\_1x2x6\_centralAPA} and \textit{vd\_1x8x14\_3view\_30deg} geometries, with a slight advantage for the former. The \textit{vd\_1x8x14\_3view\_30deg\_nominal} geometry has a worse energy resolution due to the higher background rate, but it is still acceptable for low-energy neutrino physics analyses.
