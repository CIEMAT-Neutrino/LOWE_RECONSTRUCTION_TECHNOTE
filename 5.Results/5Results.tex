\section{Results\label{sec:RESULTS}}
From the TPC cluster vertex reconstruction and the flash--matching, we can extract the energy and vertex resolution. The calibrated neutrino energy is obtained from a standalone code \href{https://github.com/CIEMAT-Neutrino/SOLAR}{SOLAR} package, which formats the simulation output into numpy arrays and performs the successive operations presented in~\cref{sec:RECONSTRUCTION}. Here we present the main figures of merit and compare the resolution performance between the two geometries, the \textit{hd\_1x2x6\_centralAPA} and the \textit{vd\_1x8x15\_3view\_30deg} and additional background reduced scenarios.
